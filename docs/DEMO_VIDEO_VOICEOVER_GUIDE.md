## Recording setup

1. Open the captioned video and read from this guide as it plays.
2. Record in a quiet room, 6–8 inches from the microphone, with the input
   meter peaking below clipping.
3. Export or share the recording as WAV, M4A, or MP3 without background music.
4. Give Codex the audio file. The final step will align it to the video and
   export the finished submission master.
