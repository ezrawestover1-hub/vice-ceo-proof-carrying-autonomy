# Vice CEO — Frederick Voiceover Script (2:07 cut)

This is the exact narration generated in ElevenLabs with Frederick Surrey's

Meet Vice CEO: a behind-the-scenes business operator.

Your business should not run on sticky notes, inbox chasing, and forgotten
follow-ups.

It watches for routine work and moves it forward before it becomes another
problem.

Not another chatbot. An operator that prepares the next useful action.

A customer asks for help resetting a password.

Vice CEO reads the request, finds the approved policy, and prepares a clear
reply.

The customer context, support policy, and proposed response stay connected and
easy to inspect.

When the business enables mailbox authority, it can send approved routine
replies through the company mailbox.

Until then, the same work waits, prepared and transparent, for review.

Outreach gets the same disciplined follow-through.

A good lead does not disappear because a busy team missed the next touch.

Vice CEO works from approved campaigns and consented contacts, then stops on
reply or unsubscribe.

No duplicate outreach. No forgotten spreadsheet tab. No good lead quietly
going cold.

And strong autonomy also knows when not to act.

A refund or exception needs context, empathy, and a business decision, so it
comes back to the owner.

The owner sees a focused decision, not another messy thread.

Every action has a receipt: its source, policy, decision, and delivery state.

That means the business can understand the work instead of blindly trusting the
AI.

The business decides exactly how much authority Vice CEO receives.

Start with preparation. Enable delivery only for approved tasks and systems.

Westover EPR is the first real-world example of the operator pattern.

It watches approved public registry signals, preserves evidence, and prepares
the next follow-through.

But the pattern reaches farther: customer service, outreach, compliance
signals, and operations.

The repeatable work moves quietly in the background, before it becomes another
inbox job.

People are brought in only for the decisions that actually need people.

Vice CEO is built for useful, controlled, real business work.

Less chasing. More running the business.
